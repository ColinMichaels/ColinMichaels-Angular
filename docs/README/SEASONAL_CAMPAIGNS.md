| Control | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `enabled: true`, `mode: 'manual'`, known `manualEditionId` | Explicit owner activation of one edition; public visitors cannot choose this setting. |
| Default `mode: 'calendar'` plus explicitly approved entries | Only approved valid non-review windows in the configured timezone select a campaign; Halloween 2026 is approved and the other 41 remain unapproved. Minute/visibility refresh rechecks dates without navigation. |
| `mode: 'off'` or `enabled: false` | Retire normal-site seasonal decoration while keeping explicit archive visits available as read-only collection guides. |
| Signed-in account bypass (`cm.seasonal-preference.v1`) | Registered signed-in accounts can use the normal design in this browser. Anonymous visitors ignore the saved bypass and follow the site; all progress is retained. |
| Holiday options / restoration | Registered signed-in accounts can opt out of or restore an active owner/date-selected presentation without losing progress. No Season selector or main-site override is exposed; the neutral archive has no idle picker. |

`/archive/seasons` is the neutral editorial index. **Find a celebration** filters the 42 editions by holiday label, year, or title and announces the result count. **Holiday options** serves only a registered signed-in account in an active-edition or restoration context; there is no public Season selector or idle Theme control on the neutral index. Archive cards deliberately open their own public edition pages without setting a main-site preference. `/archive/seasons/:id` opens only a registered edition and presents read-only hunt previews, saved Found status, and clues or quiet reflection context there. Archive clicks cannot award finds; earning an item requires its actual public hiding place while that edition is active. Unknown IDs produce a safe empty/missing state, not another season. These local routes are review artifacts until a separately authorized release includes the applicable SEO/Hosting verification.
