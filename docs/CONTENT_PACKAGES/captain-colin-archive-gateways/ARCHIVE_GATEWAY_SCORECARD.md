## Selection Rule

Raw views do not choose the winner. Each candidate receives a `0–5` score. Current-promise fit, first-party evidence, and evergreen utility receive double weight; discovery proof, continuation fit, audience conversation, and risk control receive single weight. A higher risk-control score means fewer unresolved safety, privacy, rights, or accuracy issues.

## Decision

Select **Camera ‘sham’ era! Insta360 Ace Pro First Reaction and FPV Flights video** (`OFeCTH2LP9s`) for the next evergreen gateway package.

The Ace Pro video wins because it has a complete first-party transcript and a specific unresolved audience need. The footage documents the actual mount and flights; the narration separates 4K30 Active HDR and PureVideo attempts; and viewers ask about settings, stabilization, raw versus edited footage, low light, and weight. That supports an honest field-test entry point without pretending the upload was a controlled laboratory review.

The `55K` Hurricane Wilma clip does not win merely because it is largest. It is a `21`-second severe-weather archive fragment that sends viewers to a separate full film. Confirm the master, rights, privacy, event context, and external-film ownership before changing its packaging.

Keep the Stuart Inlet video for a privacy-aware **Florida Adventures** pass. Use the BetaFPV 95X video as the selected Ace Pro video's closest next watch; it has a strong on-promise subject and the largest visible conversation among the two FPV candidates. A later visible-comment review recovered Colin's own ISO/Log/color, battery/runtime, and acro-practice answers even though the music-led video has no transcript. Those bounded details now support a separate [archive field-notes package](../betafpv-95x-v3-over-water-flight-notes/POST_PACKAGE.md) without turning the flight into a controlled review.
