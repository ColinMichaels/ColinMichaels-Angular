### 4:05–5:02 — The Part 103 boundary

**Narration**

In the United States, Part 103 provides a narrow path for an ultralight vehicle used by one occupant for sport or recreation. For a powered ultralight, the rule also sets empty-weight, top-speed, and power-off stall-speed limits.

The operating rules cover daylight, hazardous operation, congested areas and open-air assemblies, certain controlled airspace, visual reference with the surface, visibility, and cloud clearance. The Goonzquad video does not publish enough verified aircraft data for me to decide whether this machine qualified.

No pilot certificate under Part 103 does not mean no rules. It definitely does not mean flying a giant multicopter over traffic because the thumbnail looked fun.
