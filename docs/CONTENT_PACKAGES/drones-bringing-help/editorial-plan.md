## Evidence boundaries

- Sweden: use the original 2023 observational study. The 37/55 arrival comparison is a subset; 72 of 211 alerts led to drone deployment. Do not call 67% a survival benefit or all-call success rate.
- Rwanda: 2022 retrospective research, data collected through 2019. Road times were estimated. Do not describe it as a current worldwide performance guarantee or proven mortality effect.
- Search: the June 2026 report is an agency account of one rescue, not an independent trial or an AI accuracy benchmark.
- Bridges: FHWA permits supplemental use; a camera does not replace required physical examinations.
- Fire: distinguish coordinated agency operations from public flights near incidents. No tactical or operating instructions are needed.
- Privacy, noise, misuse, and reliability are legitimate concerns. Do not claim everyone fears drones or quantify public opinion without evidence.
