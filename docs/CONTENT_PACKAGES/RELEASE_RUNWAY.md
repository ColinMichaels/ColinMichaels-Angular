## Decision

The current worktree already contains eight evidence-ready article packages. The growth problem is no longer a lack of drafts; it is selecting, releasing, measuring, and learning from the right ones.

The first three operational releases should be:

1. **Temu full-size-drone stable-ID refresh** — repair the largest proven site opportunity instead of creating another URL.
2. **Insta360 Ace Pro FPV article plus existing-video gateway** — launch the strongest first-party cross-channel package.
3. **Laundry Chair** — test the promise beyond drones with a recognizable problem, useful cheaper alternatives, and a complete video package.

This sequence deliberately combines one search/CTR repair, one first-party flight-and-camera story, and one non-drone research-led household gadget. It does not call any of them a winner before comparable post-release evidence exists. A production preflight also found that HOVERAir AQUA already has a complete indexed article under a different slug, so that staged package is now consolidation-only and cannot become a second public URL.

## Measurement Gate

Record raw counts and timestamps immediately before each release. Review at 7 and 28 days:

- Search: organic impressions, clicks, CTR, position, and the exact queries/pages involved.
- Reading: 75%/95% progress, completes, saves, shares, attributed share landings, approved comments, and poll votes.
- Continuation: related-article and companion-video selections.
- Video: impressions and CTR by surface, first-30-second retention, average view duration, watch time, end-screen continuation, comments, likes, and net subscribers.
- Loyalty: new, casual, regular, and returning viewers/readers where the current tools expose them.

For the Temu page, retain the documented target of at least 2% CTR only after at least 100 new comparable impressions and without losing first-page position. The new Ace Pro and Laundry Chair articles first need clean baselines; do not invent success thresholds before traffic exists. The live HOVERAir page needs its own current GA4 and Search Console export before any demand claim.

Compare similar formats and report denominators. A view is not a completed read, an impression is not a click, a subscriber is not proof of loyalty, and a low-volume percentage is not proof of popularity.

## Stop Conditions

Pause the exact release when:

- a source, product page, order path, price, rule, or public video materially changed;
- image, footage, likeness, or third-party-media rights are unresolved;
- the production record no longer matches the stable-ID update package;
- authenticated Production Preview is unavailable or visibly diverges from the import;
- the public topic/resource foundation is not deployed and verified;
- the article-to-video destination cannot return a public 200 with the exact self-canonical;
- the evidence basis, relationship, AI assistance, or synthetic-media disclosure would be misleading.
